How a roofing visit and work are discussed

A useful first conversation starts with your observations. Tell the roofer what you’ve seen, and ask what needs to be checked to understand the situation. Leave roof access and any close inspection to a professional. Don’t climb up to take a closer look, especially if you’re unsure about the surface or conditions.

Once the concern has been assessed, ask for the recommendation in plain terms. You should be able to tell what work is proposed and what it is intended to address. If there are choices, ask what each option involves. If the explanation doesn’t answer your concern, pause and ask for clarification before approving work.

Have a way to record the main points, such as a short note on your phone. That helps you remember what was discussed and compare the explanation with any written scope you receive. Avoid treating a broad verbal description as a full plan when important details are still unclear.

What should I tell a roofer in Linden when I call?
