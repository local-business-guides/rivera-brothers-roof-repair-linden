What to ask and prepare

Roof installation involves more than choosing a new covering. A contractor needs to understand the property and the work being discussed, then explain the planned scope and what you should expect. Ask which areas are included, what preparation is needed around the home, and how the contractor will communicate about progress or unexpected findings.

A short preparation list can make the discussion easier:

  • Write down the reason you’re considering a new roof.
  • Make a list of questions about scope, timing, and access.
  • Take pictures of any visible concerns from the ground or indoors.
  • Tell the contractor about areas of the home that may need extra care during the work.
  • Review the project details and ask about anything that isn’t clear.

Don’t choose a plan based on an assumption about what is under the existing roof. Let the contractor explain what can be determined and how any new information will be handled. Avoid treating a verbal summary as a substitute for understanding the agreed scope.
