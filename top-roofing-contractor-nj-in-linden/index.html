A checklist for comparing contractors

Use the same questions with each company you contact. That makes it easier to compare what’s actually being proposed instead of relying on general impressions.

  • Does the contractor handle the type of roofing work I need?
  • What did the contractor observe, and how does that support the recommendation?
  • Which tasks and materials are included in the proposed scope?
  • What should I prepare before work starts?
  • How will I get updates or ask questions?

Ask for the work details in writing and read them before agreeing. Don’t assume that removal, cleanup, or additional repairs are included unless they’re listed. Be cautious about making a decision while important details are still unclear. You can ask for time to review the proposed plan.
