What repair can involve

The work depends on the source and extent of the trouble. A roofer may need to examine the affected area, identify how water or damage is getting through, and explain the repair approach. Don’t assume a ceiling mark tells the whole story. The visible symptom and the roof opening may not be in the same spot.

Use this short checklist before you call:

  • Write down when you noticed the issue and whether it has changed.
  • Take photos from a safe place, such as indoors or from the ground.
  • Note which room or part of the home is affected.
  • Keep the area below an active leak clear and protect belongings if you can do so safely.
  • Ask what the work includes and what to expect during the repair.

Avoid describing a cause as certain if you haven’t confirmed it. Share what you observed instead. That helps keep the conversation focused on the condition, not a guess about what caused it.
